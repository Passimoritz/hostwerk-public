### Tools

![Tools](img/projects--tools.jpg)

> Package cache A local mirror for npm, yarn, pnpm, pip and Composer: every package comes from the internet once and then stays on this PC. Installs in all projects get faster, and everything downloaded before works offline. Composer talks https to it with a certificate of the local certificate authority – other sources are still checked as usual. Turning it off restores the previous settings.
